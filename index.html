02 / COMMITMENT READINESS ASSESSMENT

Make each judgement and its weighting open to examination

The five constructs describe candidate assessment dimensions. Their illustrative proportions support prototype exploration while formal calibration requires dated commitment episodes, declared outcomes and explicit value judgements.

CANDIDATE25%

Evidence Claim Fit

Relevance, timing, independence and correspondence with the critical assumption.

CANDIDATE25%

Commercial Viability

Resources, cash capacity and delivery conditions for the contemplated action.

CANDIDATE20%

Reversibility Exposure

Persistent losses, contractual obligations and practical switching costs.

CANDIDATE15%

Strategic Optionality

Feasible alternative pathways and the cost of preserving future choice.

CANDIDATE15%

Exit Executability

Observable triggers, responsible owners and documented withdrawal authority.

Predictive contribution

Temporal validation will estimate each construct’s contribution to a specified outcome in a defined population, with sensitivity to sector, stage and observation coverage.

Decision values

Structured elicitation will establish how stakeholders value performance differences across the five constructs, with recorded disagreements and alternative weight sets.

Constraints and unknowns

Permission and unacceptable harm receive prior checks, while incomplete ratings retain an unknown state and the composite display awaits complete inputs.

Current implementation · Editable candidate weights, complete input checks and comparison with equal weights. Empirical calibration and wider sensitivity studies remain research milestones.

PHASE 01

Data and construct validity

Independent coding will examine identity links, evidence availability dates, exposure and exit rights, including agreement and excluded cases.

PHASE 02

Model validity

Temporal benchmarks will compare finance based, generic evidence, equal weight and calibrated specifications under alternative missingness assumptions.

PHASE 03

Decision utility

A comparative study will vary evidence strength and reversal cost under matched information, assessing calibration, action redesign and executable exit planning.

Evaluation state · The protocol specifies future comparative and field studies. Current evidence supports the research design and prototype mechanics, with entrepreneur outcomes awaiting observation.
