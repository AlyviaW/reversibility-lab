How much evidence is enough for this commitment?

The exposure created by a decision informs the strength and relevance of the evidence required to support it.
Core design principle

The harder a commitment is to reverse if its critical assumption fails, the stronger the evidence required. Extensive testing also carries costs through delay and foregone opportunities.
